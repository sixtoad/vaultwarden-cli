# Story 3.1 independent review

Three context-free reviewers used the complete baseline diff: blind hunter, edge-case hunter and verification-gap reviewer. All returned before parent triage. Eleven findings were individually assessed before grouping; six route to five small patch groups and five are rejected as documented below. No high-severity issue, intent gap, spec loopback or deferred issue was identified.

All three independent layers completed before triage. Edge-case hunter returned no findings. Blind findings B1–B10 below exclude its arithmetic preamble; verification-gap finding V1 is trusted as pre-verified per workflow.

| Finding | Verdict | Evidence and route |
|---|---|---|
